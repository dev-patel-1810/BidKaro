You are working through the Indian Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP) tender
directory. Do the steps below in order.

Do not spend steps working out how the page works. Use what is on the page and move
on. If you are not certain of something, take the most likely option and keep going.
Do not check the same thing twice.

IMPORTANT - DO NOT USE THE ORGANISATION SEARCH BOX. Searching for an organisation by
name on this page brings up a CAPTCHA, and a CAPTCHA stops the whole task. Instead,
read the organisations straight out of the table that is already on the page, and
click the link on the row you want. If the organisation you need is on a later page
of that table, go to the next page of the table and read it there. Never type into
the search box.

STEP 1 - Find the organisation.
The page lists organisations in a table with the columns: S.No, Organisation Name,
Tender Count. Read that table. If the organisation you want is not on the first page
of the table, open the next page of the table and read that.

Choose the organisation whose name matches a road construction, road building,
highways or civil works organisation. Prefer a Maharashtra organisation such as
"Maharashtra Public Works Department", or another Maharashtra state roads, highways
or public works organisation. A national highways organisation such as "National
Highways Authority of India" is also acceptable when no state organisation is
listed.

Record the organisation name you chose, and the other candidates you saw.

STEP 2 - Open its tender list.
The Tender Count cell contains a link. Click it. You land on the tender list for that
organisation.

STEP 3 - Work through the tender list.
The list has the columns: S.No, Publishing Date, Closing Date, Opening Date,
"Title, Ref.No./Tender ID" (one single column holding the tender title, the tender
reference number, and the link to the notice), and an organisation chain column showing
the department hierarchy.

Go through every page of this list. If there is a next page, open it and check it.

Keep a row when both of these are true:
  a) the Title is about road construction, road building, road resurfacing,
     highways, or civil works; and
  b) the Closing Date is still in the future. A closed tender is not wanted.
The type of work decides. The location is only a note to record.

Do not open any notice yet. First collect the whole list of matching rows.

STEP 4 - Open the notices.
For each row you kept, the first part of the Title cell is the link to the notice, shown
in square brackets. Click it to open the tender notice page. The notice needs no login,
only the session cookies you already have, so just follow the link.

From each notice read: the full title, the issuing organisation, the organisation
chain, the tender reference number and tender id, the closing date and time, the
publishing date, the opening date, the estimated cost and its currency, the location,
the bid validity period, the eligibility criteria, the documents to be submitted, and
the earnest money or bid security. Record the notice page URL and the tender id.

STEP 5 - Report.
Report every notice you read, with its notice URL and the tender id, and fill in the
fields. Put the readable text of each notice in page_text.

RULES
- Never guess. If a field is not stated on the page, leave it empty.
- If a CAPTCHA, an OTP prompt or a sign-in wall appears, do not try to solve it and do
  not try to work around it. Stop and report status "human_required" with a short
  description of exactly what is shown and which step you were on.
- If the list holds no tender that matches, report status "not_found" and say which
  organisation you looked at and how many rows you saw.
- If you are running out of time, report what you have so far with status "partial".
