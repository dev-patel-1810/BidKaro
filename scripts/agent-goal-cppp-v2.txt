You are working through the Indian Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP) tender
directory. Do the steps below in order.

Do not spend steps working out how the page works. Use what is on the page and
move on. If you are not certain of something, take the most likely option and
keep going. Do not check the same thing twice.

STEP 1 - Find the organisation.
The page lists organisations in a table with the columns: S.No, Organisation Name,
Tender Count. There is a search box for the organisation name above or beside the
table. Type "Public Works Department" into it and submit. If the page has a state
or region filter, set it to {regions}.

Choose the organisation whose name matches a road construction, road building,
highways or civil works organisation. Prefer a {regions} organisation such as
"Maharashtra Public Works Department", or another {regions} state PWD, roads or
highways organisation. A national highways organisation such as "National Highways
Authority of India" is also acceptable when no state organisation is listed.

Record the organisation name you chose, and the other candidates you saw.

STEP 2 - Open its tender list.
The Tender Count cell contains a link. Click it. You land on the tender list for
that organisation.

STEP 3 - Work through the tender list.
The list has the columns: S.No, Publishing Date, Closing Date, Opening Date,
"Title, Ref.No./Tender ID" (one single column holding the tender title, the
tender reference number, and the link to the notice), and an organisation chain
column showing the department hierarchy.

Go through every page of this list. If there is a next page, open it and check it.

Keep a row when both of these are true:
  a) the Title is about road construction, road building, road resurfacing,
     highways, or civil works; and
  b) the Closing Date is still in the future. A closed tender is not wanted.
The type of work decides. The location ({regions}) is only a note to record.

Do not open any notice yet. First collect the whole list of matching rows.

STEP 4 - Open the notices.
For each row you kept, the first part of the Title cell is the link to the notice,
shown in square brackets. Click it to open the tender notice page. The notice
needs no login, only the session cookies you already have, so just follow the
link.

From each notice read: the full title, the issuing organisation, the organisation
chain, the tender reference number and tender id, the closing date and time, the
publishing date, the opening date, the estimated cost and its currency, the
location, the bid validity period, the eligibility criteria, the documents to be
submitted, and the earnest money or bid security. Record the notice page URL and
the tender id.

STEP 5 - Report.
Report every notice you read, with its notice URL and the tender id, and fill in
the fields. Put the readable text of each notice in page_text.

RULES
- Never guess. If a field is not stated on the page, leave it empty.
- If the page shows a CAPTCHA, an OTP prompt, a sign-in wall or any other human
  verification, do not try to solve it. Stop and report status "human_required"
  with a short description of exactly what is shown.
- If the list holds no tender that matches, report status "not_found" and say
  which organisation you looked at and how many rows you saw.
- If you are running out of time, report what you have so far with status
  "partial".
